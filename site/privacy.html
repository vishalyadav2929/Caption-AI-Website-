Privacy

Your videos never leave your device. Uploading, transcribing, editing and exporting all happen inside your browser. There is no account, no database, no analytics, no ads and no cookies.

Outside requests the site makes

ServiceWhenWhat is sent
Netlify (hosting)Every page loadA normal web request for the site’s files. Netlify keeps standard server logs.
Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com)Every page loadRequests for the caption fonts. Google sees your IP address and browser, as with any web font.
jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net)Only when you click “Generate captions”Downloads the open-source transformers.js library and its WebAssembly runtime.
Hugging Face (huggingface.co)Only the first time you generate captions with a given qualityDownloads the Whisper speech model (about 40–250 MB). Your browser caches it, so later runs don’t download it again. Your audio is not sent; the model runs on your device.
MyMemory (api.mymemory.translated.net)Only when you press “Translate” in a browser without a built-in translator (e.g. iPhone Safari)The caption text only, to be translated. Never your video or audio. Hindi ↔ Hinglish is done on your device and sends nothing.
Your browser’s speech serviceOnly in “Live captions” modeLive captions use your browser’s built-in speech recognition. In Chrome and Edge this sends microphone audio to Google or Microsoft to be transcribed. Uploaded videos never use this.

What stays on your device

  • The video or audio file you choose. It is opened locally and never uploaded.
  • The transcript, your caption edits and style settings. They live in this browser tab and are gone when you close it.
  • The exported video, which your browser saves straight to your downloads.
